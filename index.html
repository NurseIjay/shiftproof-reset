The origin story

I built this because I needed it first.

I was a travel nurse working rotating shifts on brutal hospital assignments, trying my best to be a present father to two young kids who deserved more energy than I had to give.

I felt like I was surviving every day, even though I was doing all the "right" things. Cutting carbs. Eating "healthy." Moving from the second I clocked in to the second I clocked out. Juices. Intermittent fasting. I was hardly eating and putting in all this effort despite being bone-tired.

But my scrubs were still getting tighter. My face was puffy. My stomach was bloated whether I ate or not. I was so exhausted I felt like a ghost in my own life, to my own kids, and running on empty for the patients who needed me clear-headed.

I looked in the mirror one morning after a brutal shift and thought, "This can't be it. I help people heal all day, but I'm breaking."

So I did what we all do. I doubled down. I hired a coach who gave me a strict macro plan and told me to do more HIIT. I lasted three weeks before I crashed, bingeing on everything in the pantry and feeling like a complete failure.

That failure was a blessing. It forced me to use my nursing background to look at my own physiology. I stopped asking about calories and started asking how to escape being trapped in fight-or-flight. I started asking what true healing really is.

I realized my body wasn't resisting weight loss. It was resisting stress. I built a simple, 7-day protocol focused on one thing: calming the system. I ate more of the right foods. I moved less. In one week, the puffiness was gone, my energy was back, and I had lost 5 pounds.

I hadn't found a new diet. I had found a new way to finally feel like myself again. I started sharing it with the other nurses on my unit. The results were immediate and universal. Now, over 400 nurses have used this exact reset to get their bodies back on their side.
